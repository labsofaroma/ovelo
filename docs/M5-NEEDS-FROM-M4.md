# M5 needs from M4: resolved

_M5a (creators, the wallet-independent part) was built on `m5-creators`, cut from `m3-read-only-app`, while M4 was in progress. M5b rebased `m5-creators` onto `m4-wallet-trading` (14 November 2026) and cleared every item below. Commit hashes are on the rebased `m5-creators` branch. Nothing is pushed or merged._

| # | Need | Resolved in M5b by | Commit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`fast-check`** as a dev dependency | M4 installed `fast-check` 4.10.2. `server/auth/creator.property.test.ts` now runs the same seven properties as `fc.assert(fc.asyncProperty(...))`, 200 runs each; the seeded `mulberry32` generator is gone. | `26d1734` |
| 2 | **Origin check and rate limits in `middleware.ts`** | The per-handler `assertSameOrigin()` (whose `request.url` fallback had M4's `next start` bug) and the handler limiters (one shared `"local"` bucket without a trusted proxy) are deleted. M4's middleware enforces the Origin allowlist on every `/api/*` route; a new `creator` bucket (10/min) covers challenge issuance and invite redemption. The challenge's `Site:` line now uses middleware's `requestProtocol`/`requestHost`. Tests: `server/middleware.creators.test.ts`, `server/request-guard.test.ts`. | `44672c7`, `a5cb003`, `332abae` |
| 3 | **`useSigner()` from `features/wallet`** | `Signer.signMessage(bytes)` (Wallet Standard `solana:signMessage`, legacy adapter `signMessage`, Privy `useSignMessage`, the E2E bridge in test builds only; wallets that cannot sign messages are refused with a clear message). `BuilderForm` publishes through `PublishPanel`: connect → redeem the invite (first time) → challenge → sign → submit, with explicit steps; archive is behind a confirmation dialog on the basket page. The disabled **Publish (connect wallet)** button is gone. | `31c9b68`, `8daa1cf` |
| 4 | **Purchase receipts** | M4's trade server now quotes and sells published creator baskets and writes `receipts.basket_id` (curated stays null) with the basket version on every leg. `features/creators/stats.ts` counts purchases (orders), unique buyers and raw volume per funding mint from **confirmed** receipts only, over 7 days, 30 days and all time, with an as-of time; no confirmed purchase is "No purchases yet", never 0. Purchases through the v1 Solana Action are recorded by its `links.next` callback after the transaction is read back from mainnet. | `c33f74a`, `3c092a9`, `c34ee5d` |
| 5 | **Migration numbering** | `0002_basket_versions` (after M4's `0000`/`0001`): an insert-only history of every published version (triggers refuse UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE), written in the publish transaction. Receipt legs point at `(basket_id, version)`. | `9e4e0f3` |
| 6 | **A Postgres reachable by `@neondatabase/serverless` locally** | `apps/web/scripts/local-db.sh` (`pnpm --filter @aroma/web db:local up`): Docker Postgres 17 + Neon's open-source WebSocket proxy, migrations applied through the production driver. `server/db/local-neon.ts` only applies to a loopback `DATABASE_URL` with `AROMA_LOCAL_NEON_WS_PROXY` set, never in Vercel production. The fork E2E resets and uses it. | `f4e67c9` |

## The rebase heads-up items

- **`tsconfig.strict.json`** brace-glob fix: after the rebase, M4's `features/{wallet,trade,portfolio}` files passed the strict check unchanged (`pnpm typecheck` green on the rebased branch). No fixes were needed.
- **Creator routes** folded into `features/shared/routes.ts` (`creators`, `builder`); `features/creators/routes.ts` keeps named aliases. The nav has a **Creators** link. (`8daa1cf`)
- **`CREATOR_AUTH_SECRET` and `CREATOR_INVITE_CODES`** are still prototype-only and are removed at M7 with the prototype routes (unchanged).
