## Heads-up for the rebase (not needs, but collisions to expect)

- **`apps/web/tsconfig.strict.json`** was fixed on this branch: its `include` used brace globs (`features/**/*.{ts,tsx}`, `app/(app)/**/*.{ts,tsx}`), which TypeScript does not expand, so those folders were only strictly checked when imported from elsewhere. With one pattern per extension, all 100 existing `features/` files pass. After the rebase, **M4's new `features/{wallet,trade,portfolio}` files will be strictly checked too** (`noUncheckedIndexedAccess`), and may need small fixes.
- **Creator routes** are in `features/creators/routes.ts`, not `features/shared/routes.ts`, and there is no nav link to `/v1/creators` or `/v1/basket/new` yet. Fold them into `routes` and `NavLinks` once both branches are in.
- **`CREATOR_AUTH_SECRET` and `CREATOR_INVITE_CODES`** (env schema, `.env.example`) are prototype-only. The v1 flow needs neither: challenges are single-use nonces in Redis, and invites are hashed rows in Postgres. Remove them at M7 along with the prototype routes.
